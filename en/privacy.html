Privacy Policy

Out of Office – the escape · Last updated: 12 October 2026

Data controller: Out Of Office Dev Team. For any questions: outofofficedevteam@gmail.com.

1. About this game

The game has no user accounts, chat, multiplayer or social features, and we run no server that receives game data. The only information that leaves your device is what Google services process to show ads (section 3) and to handle purchases (section 5).

2. Age

The game is meant for people aged 13 or over. It is not directed at children. On first launch we ask for your year of birth (or “I’d rather not say”) only to work out an age band; we store only the band (under 13 or 13+), never the date or the year.

If you say you are under 13, or give no age, the game runs in a restricted mode:

  • no banners and no ads between levels;
  • optional rewarded ads stay available (they only appear if you tap to watch one), always non-personalised, requested with the child-directed and under-age-of-consent tags and content suitable for all ages. To serve them, the ad provider processes technical data (IP address, device and app information; see section 3), but does not use it for personalisation or profiling;
  • no statistics;
  • an adult must confirm before purchases, restoring purchases and external links.

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 beyond the technical processing described above, done by the ad provider. If you think we have, please contact us.

3. Ads

The game shows ads through Google AdMob, and consent is collected with Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP). No ad is requested before the consent form has answered.

When ads are requested (personalised or not), Google’s SDK may collect:

  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it (the game does not ask for location permission);
  • device identifiers: the advertising ID and the App Set ID;
  • device and app information (model, OS, language, app version);
  • interactions with ads (views, taps);
  • diagnostics and crash logs from the SDK itself;
  • on Android, the Privacy Sandbox APIs (Topics and Attribution), where the system has them enabled.

Google uses this data to show and measure ads, prevent fraud and keep the service running. In restricted mode (section 2) and when you refuse personalisation, ads are non-personalised.

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, we ask for your consent before personalised ads. In those countries you can review your choice in Settings > Review consent.
  • Rewarded ads (to get a help item or double a level reward) are always optional.
  • You can delete or limit the advertising ID in your system settings (Android/iOS); the SDK respects that choice.
  • Buying “Remove ads” removes banners and ads between levels; rewarded ads remain available because they are optional.

More information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

4. Game data stays on your device

This version sends no statistics or game data to any server. Everything the game stores stays only on your device:

  • progress: unlocked levels, stars, scores, best times, medals and episodes already watched;
  • the results of each attempt while you play (time, alarm, tricks used, help items used);
  • settings: volume, language, graphics quality, accessibility, vibration and the episode-scene options;
  • your character: the name you choose (up to 12 characters, shown only in the game), body type, outfit and colours;
  • in-game currency (clips), cosmetics bought or earned, and unused help items;
  • the tokens of purchases already processed, so the same purchase is never credited twice;
  • your age band and consent choices;
  • the “Statistics” option in Settings and the random installation code linked to it. In this version, even when switched on, nothing is sent: the events the game prepares (session start, level start and end with stars, time, alarm and tricks, failures and their cause, help items, purchases, graphics quality, tutorial, episode scenes started, skipped or watched, app version and platform) are discarded and not stored.

We do not send this data to us or to third parties. If you delete the app, it is gone. If a future version starts sending statistics, we will update this policy first and ask for your separate consent (off by default, never in restricted mode).

5. Purchases

Purchases are made through Google Play (Android) and the App Store (iOS), when available. Payment is handled only by the store: we do not receive your payment details. The store keeps your purchase history and shows us the order in its console (product, date, country, amount).

Each purchase confirmation is received and checked by the app itself, on your device: we have no purchase server. Consumables (clips, help items) are credited only after the store confirms.

Restoring purchases: if you reinstall the game or move to another device with the same store account, tap Restore purchases in the Shop or in Settings. Permanent purchases are restored (for example “Remove ads”, the Bonus Pack and premium outfits); consumables already used are not. In restricted mode, restoring asks for an adult’s confirmation.

6. Legal bases

  • Personalised ads (and device access for them): your consent, collected by the UMP; you can withdraw it at any time, without affecting processing already done.
  • Non-personalised ads (13+ users who refuse personalisation, and rewarded ads in restricted mode): legitimate interest in funding a free game and preventing ad fraud, limited to technical data and with no profiling; where the law requires consent to store or read information on the device, that consent is asked by the UMP.
  • Purchases and delivering what you bought: performance of a contract.
  • Data stored only on the device: not processed by us.

7. Who we share data with

Only with Google (AdMob and UMP, for ads and consent; Google Play, for purchases) and Apple (App Store, for purchases), once the game is on the App Store. Google acts as an independent controller (Google Ireland Limited in the European Economic Area and Switzerland; Google LLC in the United Kingdom). Some data may be processed outside the European Economic Area, in the United States, under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (and the Swiss-U.S. Framework and the UK Extension), under which Google LLC is certified.

8. Retention

Data on your device stays until you delete the app. Data processed by Google follows Google’s retention periods. We keep no game data.

9. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, portability and to withdraw consent at any time. As we have no server, game data is erased by deleting the app; consent is changed in Settings; for data processed by Google you can also use Google’s tools (for example, deleting the advertising ID). For any request, write to outofofficedevteam@gmail.com. You can complain to a supervisory authority: in Portugal, the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD).

10. Security

Data sent to Google travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Local data stays on your device, protected by the system.

11. Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we will tell you in the app. The current version is always on this page. Versão em português.
