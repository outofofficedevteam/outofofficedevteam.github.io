Draft in preparation. This policy will be reviewed and finalised before the game is released. Highlighted fields are still to be completed.

1. Who we are

Data controller: Out Of Office Dev Team (independent developer). Contact: outofficedevteam@gmail.com. The controller's full identification is shown on the app page on Google Play and the App Store and can be requested by e-mail.

2. What stays on your device

The game has no accounts, chat or social features. It stores only on your device: progress, scores, settings (volume, language, quality), wardrobe and in-game currency, the name you choose for your character (up to 12 characters, shown only in the game), your character's body choice, and your age range (under 13 or 13 and over, no date of birth). This data is not sent to us. If you uninstall the game, it is gone.

3. Age

The game is intended for people aged 13 or over and is not directed at children. If you say you are under 13, or do not give your age, the game runs in a restricted mode: no personalised ads, no statistics, and an adult confirmation before purchases. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13; if you believe this happened, contact us and we will delete it.

4. Ads

The game shows ads through [AdMob, by Google]. The provider may collect device data, such as the advertising identifier, IP address, device information and ad interactions, to show and measure ads and prevent fraud. In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom we ask for your consent before showing personalised ads; you can change it at any time in the settings. Rewarded ads (to get a boost or double a reward) are always optional. Buying “Remove ads” removes banners and ads between levels.

5. Usage statistics (optional)

With your consent we collect pseudonymised usage events to improve the game: level start and end, which level and why you fail, which boosts you use, purchases, graphics quality and tutorial completion. We use a random installation code, which you can reset in the settings; we do not collect your name, free text, contacts, location or the advertising identifier for this purpose. Provider: [analytics provider]. Retention period: [period]. You can turn statistics off in the settings. In restricted mode there are no statistics.

6. Purchases

Purchases are made through [Google Play / App Store]. We receive confirmation of the purchase (which product and when), not your payment details. Purchases are never required to finish the game.

7. Legal bases

Personalised ads and statistics: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time without affecting processing already done. Purchases: performance of a contract. Security and fraud prevention in ads: [to be confirmed with the provider].

8. Sharing and transfers

We share data only with the providers above ([ad provider], [analytics provider] and the app store). Some may process data outside the European Economic Area, with the safeguards provided by law [to be confirmed].

9. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, portability and to withdraw consent. To exercise them, write to outofficedevteam@gmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority: in Portugal, the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD).

10. Security

Data sent to providers travels over encrypted connections. Local data stays on your device, protected by the operating system.

If we change this policy in a significant way, we will tell you in the app. The current version is always on this page. Versão em português.
