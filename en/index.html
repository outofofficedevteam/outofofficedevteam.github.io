Out of Office – the escape

Escape the office without anyone noticing.

A satirical stealth game about office life. Hide behind shelves, spill the coffee, trigger the blue screen and walk out the door before six.

Coming soon toGoogle PlayComing soon to theApp Store

The game

Stealth with a sense of humor

Vision cones, distractions and an HR team that keeps asking how you are feeling.

Episodes, like a TV series

Every level opens with an animated mini-scene to set the scene. There are 5 seasons available, with different difficulty levels. More content is coming soon.

Your colleague, your way

Pick the character, the hats, the glasses and the trail you leave behind as you run.

The team

We are the Out Of Office Dev Team, and Out of Office – the escape is our first work. It started from a simple idea: everyone has stared at the exit door on a Friday afternoon.

We like games that make sense in a minute, make you smile and respect your time. The game is free, with optional ads and purchases that are never needed to finish it.
